Focus Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

The short version

  • Focus Desk is a study planner, focus timer and app blocker for Android and Windows.
  • If you sign in with Google, your plan is kept in a private space only your account can open, so it syncs between your devices. If you don't sign in, everything stays on your device.
  • On Android, blocking uses the Accessibility service. It only looks at which app is open, web addresses in your browser and app names in your recent apps. The only thing it keeps is a note of what it blocked and when.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored in the cloud at any time, from the app.

Android: how blocking uses Accessibility

To keep you off the apps and websites on your block list, Focus Desk uses Android's Accessibility service, which you switch on yourself after Focus Desk explains it. It acts only while blocking is on, and it:

  • checks which app is open on your screen;
  • reads the web address at the top of your browser, to spot blocked websites;
  • reads app names in your recent apps, to clear blocked apps from there;
  • if you turn on the Settings protection, checks whether Focus Desk's own page in Settings is open, so blocking can't be switched off during a lock.

It closes blocked apps, backs out of blocked websites and clears blocked apps from your recent apps. It doesn't look at what you type, your messages, passwords or what's on the pages you visit. The only thing it records is a line in your focus log saying what was blocked and when. You can switch it off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings, except during a lock if you've chosen the Settings protection.

Other phone permissions

  • Notifications, to tell you when a session or break ends and when a planned block starts.
  • Alarms & reminders, so those alerts arrive on time with the screen off.
  • Do Not Disturb access, only if you choose to silence notifications while blocking.
  • Internet, for signing in and syncing.

Who processes your data

If you sign in, your data is stored by Google's Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), in Google's London data centre (europe-west2). Google processes it on Focus Desk's behalf and may keep technical records, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service. See Firebase's privacy information. Signing in uses Google's own sign-in, under Google's Privacy Policy.

Nobody else receives your data. It isn't sold, rented or used for advertising.

How your data is protected

  • Data travels between your devices and the cloud over encrypted connections, and Google encrypts it where it's stored.
  • Only someone signed in to your Google account can read or change your Focus Desk data.
  • On Android, your sign-in isn't included in phone backups or transfers to a new phone.

Legal basis

Focus Desk uses your data to provide the service you've asked for: your plan, timer, blocking and, if you sign in, sync between your devices. The Accessibility service is used only after you agree to it in the app and switch it on.

How long data is kept

Cloud data is kept while you have an account. The synced focus log covers the last 120 days. When you delete your account, your cloud data is deleted straight away. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall Focus Desk.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Focus Desk, go to Settings > Sync, tap Delete my account, then tap it again to confirm. This deletes your Focus Desk account and everything it stored in the cloud, at once and for good. Your plan stays on the device you used, and your other devices stop syncing.

Without the app: email zaininspire@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Focus Desk account". Your account and all its cloud data will be deleted within 30 days, and you'll get a reply when it's done.

To remove data from a device, uninstall Focus Desk. On Windows, also delete the Focus Desk folder in %APPDATA%.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, ask for it in a portable format, and object to how it's used. Email zaininspire@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

Focus Desk is meant for students aged 13 and over. It isn't directed at younger children and doesn't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has signed in, email us and we'll delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app.
